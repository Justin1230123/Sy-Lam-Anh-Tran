BUSINESS ANALYSIS ERP PROCESS IMPROVEMENT

Retail Store Operations & ERP Transformation

End-to-End Business Analysis Case Study

Business Challenge

A fashion retail organisation was experiencing fragmented inventory, sales, CRM and purchasing processes. Store staff had limited visibility of stock across locations, while stock allocation and replenishment decisions relied heavily on manual processes and judgement.

My Approach

Stakeholder Analysis Requirements Elicitation AS-IS Mapping Root Cause Analysis Gap Analysis Requirements TO-BE Design Fit-Gap Analysis

Key Business Requirements

  • Provide visibility of inventory across stores and warehouses.
  • Allow staff to search network-wide stock and request transfers.
  • Support CRM-based customer follow-up and purchase history.
  • Improve replenishment decisions using sales and inventory data.
  • Standardise operational workflows and reduce reliance on key staff.

Solution Evaluation

Odoo was evaluated against the identified business requirements. Standard capabilities across Inventory, Sales, CRM, Purchasing, reordering rules and stock transfers were considered as part of the fit-gap analysis.

Expected Business Impact

Improve inventory visibility, support demand-driven stock allocation, enable cross-store transfers, strengthen CRM follow-up and reduce reliance on manual processes.
